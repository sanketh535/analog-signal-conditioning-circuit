Analog Signal Conditioning Circuit

Project Overview

This project involves the design and hardware implementation of a multi-stage analog signal conditioning circuit using BJT amplification and RC filters.

The circuit is designed to process an input sinusoidal signal using a high-pass filter, a BJT common-emitter amplifier, and a low-pass filter.

Main Features

• RC high-pass filter with a cutoff frequency of 2 kHz.
• BJT common-emitter amplifier operating with a 9 V supply.
• RC low-pass filter with a cutoff frequency of 12 kHz.
• Multi-stage analog signal conditioning.
• Physical circuit implementation on a dot board.
• Oscilloscope measurements at different input frequencies.

Tools and Components

• Proteus Design Suite
• BJT transistor
• Resistors and capacitors
• Dot board
• Oscilloscope
• 9 V DC power supply

Oscilloscope Readings

Oscilloscope measurements were taken at three different input frequencies to observe the circuit's frequency response.

• 500 Hz – Low-frequency region: Below the high-pass filter cutoff frequency of 2 kHz.
• 8 kHz – Passband region: Between the high-pass and low-pass cutoff frequencies.
• 15 kHz – High-frequency region: Above the low-pass filter cutoff frequency of 12 kHz.

The readings are available in the Oscilloscope_Readings folder.

The circuit was physically constructed on a dot board. Images of the hardware implementation are available in the Prototype folder.
